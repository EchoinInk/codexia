# Future Capabilities Roadmap

Status: Unscheduled possibilities after the completed numbered Phase 8 roadmap.

## Long-Term Vision

Codexia should evolve into an AI-native development environment.

This document does not define or commit to a Phase 9. Released Phase 8
capabilities are recorded in `docs/roadmap/phases.md`; the items below describe
possible extensions that are not part of the `v0.8.6` release.


## Intelligence Expansion

Future capabilities:

- deeper cross-workspace and cross-module reasoning beyond the current
  workspace-scoped Intelligence contracts
- broader analysis-provider coverage, confidence modelling, and product-level
  visualisation of architecture, trends, and technical debt
- richer retention, correction, and policy controls for released project
  evolution memory


## Integration and Deployment

Potential capabilities:

- transport-specific IDE protocols and editor extensions over the released
  in-process platform contract
- deployment authentication and authorization supplied by a hosting environment
- provider credential acquisition and secure credential lifecycle management
- remote service configuration and administration
- a plugin marketplace or broader third-party extension ecosystem
- remote or federated workspace coordination beyond current workspace isolation


## Developer Experience and Operations

Potential capabilities:

- broader Control Centre and workspace-intelligence UI coverage
- richer notification, filtering, audit, and multi-workspace operational views
- additional policy templates and scheduling interfaces for the released
  continuous-engineering foundations
- product integrations that enter through the released governed engineering,
  lifecycle, Intelligence, and Reporter boundaries


## Guiding Principle

Autonomy should increase only as understanding and verification improve.

Future integrations must preserve local/private defaults, workspace isolation,
explicit authority, bounded execution, and Validator-confirmed outcomes.
